# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior & Analytics Hub

> **Project Reference:** Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis  
> **Target Audience:** Recruiters, Data Analysts, and Business Stakeholders  
> **Core Objective:** Uncover insights from 3,900 purchase transactions to guide strategic retail decisions, optimize marketing, and improve customer retention.

---

## 📌 Executive Summary
This project analyzes comprehensive customer shopping data spanning 18 behavioral and demographic variables. By leveraging Python data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and visualization techniques, the analysis answers critical business questions regarding gender revenue contribution, shipping preferences, subscription impacts, and customer segmentation.

---

## 📊 Key Findings & Insights

1. **Shipping Method Impact:**  
   * Customers utilizing **Express Shipping** spend an average of **$65** per transaction, compared to **$58** for Standard Shipping (a 12% increase).
2. **Subscription Value:**  
   * Subscribers account for **45% of total revenue** and demonstrate a **78% repeat-purchase loyalty rate**, outperforming non-subscribers significantly.
3. **Customer Segmentation:**  
   * Segmented into **Loyal (15%)**, **Returning (35%)**, and **New (50%)** buyers, highlighting a clear growth opportunity to convert first-time buyers into loyal brand advocates.
4. **Top-Performing Categories:**  
   * High customer satisfaction and top ratings were concentrated in apparel lines like blouses, dresses, and shirts.
